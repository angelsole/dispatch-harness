# <project> — art bible

## What it is

One paragraph: what the picture *is* (setting, hour, weather) and what the
objects on screen *mean*. State the metaphor once — a renderer that forgets it
draws decoration.

## Pillars

Three or four lines. A pillar is a sentence you can reject an asset with.

## Palette

`.creative/palette.png` — N colours, one row of 1x1 swatches, the only lock
artefact. List the hexes in groups (night and stone, warm light, cold light,
signage) with what each group is for, and say which came from design tokens in
code and which were sampled from the references.

## Camera and light

Where the eye is, the projection (side / low top-down / high top-down /
isometric), where light comes from, the shadow rule. Name the one thing that may
glow and the two that may not. Scale lives in `.creative/proportions.md`.

## Do / Don't

Two short lists. Each line must be checkable against a frame — "less black than
the reference" is a rule; "atmospheric" is not.

## References

`.creative/refs/` — what each still is, and which is the reigning look.

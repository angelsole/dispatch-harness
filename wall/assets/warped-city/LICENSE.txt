The sprites in this directory come from two free asset packs by the same author,
Luis Zuno (@ansimuz). Both are distributed as public domain / CC0 on OpenGameArt.
Credit is not required by either licence; it is given in wall/THIRD_PARTY.md and
here anyway. Only the sprite frames the wall actually draws were taken — no
music, no source files. The two licence texts follow verbatim.

Nothing else in this directory is ours: `people.png`, `vehicles.png` and
`signs.png` are the artist's pixels, repacked into one atlas each. The `.json`
files beside them are packing metadata (which rectangle is which frame), written
by the packer named in wall/THIRD_PARTY.md.
